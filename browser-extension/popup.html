Provcheck AI Blur

Hide/blur media when:
C2PA and AI use local byte-scan presence detection (not full signature validation). Nothing leaves your browser.
About: what this catches

It reads provenance and platform labels, not pixels. It can only flag media that carries a signal.

Catches:

  • C2PA provenance present or absent (byte-scan of the file).
  • AI declared in C2PA (the trainedAlgorithmicMedia assertion).
  • YouTube's own "Altered or synthetic content" AI label.
  • A keyword in the media's alt text, title, or captions.

Will not catch:

  • Unlabelled AI images with no provenance, such as raw output on AI-art sites. It cannot tell AI from pixels.
  • Content a platform publishes without the machine-readable AI mark that EU AI Act Article 50 has required since 2 August 2026. Platforms that ignore the law leave no signal to read.
  • A stream delivered as a blob, such as a YouTube player video. It reads file bytes, not streams.
  • Provenance a platform stripped by re-encoding an upload.
  • Thumbnails a site serves with the C2PA stripped: many sites re-encode an image into their own thumbnail and drop the provenance, even when the original was signed, so the thumbnail you see reads as unsigned.

Presence detection, not signature validation. Local only, nothing leaves your browser.
