# v1.5.0: feature-complete declaration

**Multi-format verification, a browser extension, thirty-language localization, tamper
evidence, and a validated keyed-mark toolset.**

This is a capability release. The provcheck verifier and creator kit stay Apache-2.0, and
the keyed marks (Backfire, Mellin, idmark) stay separate, opt-in, BUSL-1.1 tools that are
never bundled into or linked against the shipped Apache binary. The licensing line is read
versus write: reading and verifying any mark is free, and only embedding with our own keyed
codecs requires the paid layer.

## Verification and provenance (Apache-2.0, in the shipped product)

- **Unified multi-format reader.** A single format-check data model and capability registry
  drive detection across modalities, so the verifier reports a consistent verdict shape
  regardless of the input format.
- **Tamper evidence.** A C2PA-gap flag (`provenance_tamper`) surfaces when a manifest is
  present but broken, with a dedicated badge on the GUI verify result.
- **Browser extension.** A Chrome and Firefox extension detects C2PA Content Credentials and
  AI presence via a background worker, shows a provenance badge on hover, and adds a
  right-click panel to inspect a media element's provenance in place.
- **Thirty-language localization.** The GUI, CLI, backend, and ComfyUI nodes are localized
  across thirty languages.
- **ComfyUI nodes.** Detect, Sign-and-Provenance, a free-watermark node, and a Backfire
  pointer, with a standalone-repo entrypoint and an install test gate.

## Keyed marks (BUSL-1.1, opt-in, never bundled)

- **Backfire image and video.** A multi-band identifier, opt-in geometry-recovery reading,
  a content-binding forgery resistance term, a realtime CPU-portable fast mode, selective
  N-frame video keying with quorum, native-resolution video, a visibility-to-robustness level
  preset over the energy and masking knobs, and quantization-aware embed so quiet marks survive
  a lossless save.
- **License-clean default.** The default Backfire embed is now the permissive neural-codec
  fixed point, so it loads no third-party diffusion weights at all. Measured before shipping
  the default, over a 200-image corpus read through the reader at its default threshold:
  survives real regeneration 200 of 200, survives JPEG, half-size resize, and blur 200 of
  200, with zero false positives over 5,000 unmarked images. The diffusion fixed point
  remains available as an opt-in path with bring-your-own weights.
- **Per-copy tracing.** Tardos collusion tracing accuses exactly the colluders across the
  practical two- and three-way regime with zero false accusation, over the validation set.
- **idmark.** An input-scale search so downscaled and resized copies still read, with zero
  false positives over the calibration set.
- **Credential verification.** A dual-backend, per-copy content-bound credential path
  (offline registry and DID-anchored), opt-in.

## Validation at scale

Two validation windows, on a 1,600 and a 1,919 image COCO corpus, each read through the
shipped reader at its default threshold, with 95% Clopper-Pearson confidence intervals:

- **The visible seal survives the diffusion removers.** The `--level maximum` seal
  (multi-band, luminance-only, a measured 30 dB) reads back on 98.4% of 1,600 images after a
  CtrlRegen regeneration, the attack that rebuilds the image from noise, 95% CI [97.6, 98.9],
  and 99.9% after a neural-codec strip, at zero false positives over 2,000 unmarked images.
  Its visibility is stated as a number and not asserted away: SSIM 0.79, LPIPS 0.20. The
  scope is automated stripping and recompression, not a heavy manual repaint: a strong Stable
  Diffusion img2img pass erodes it, 80.0% at strength 0.20 and 50.7% at 0.30.
- **The invisible mark's everyday proof extends to roughly 1,900 images.** A 1,919-image run
  recovers the identifier on 100% of images after JPEG (quality 90 to 20), WebP, half-size
  resize, blur, noise, grayscale, a crop with register, and a neural-codec strip, at zero
  false positives over 2,000 unmarked images. The diffusion-amplification and four-model
  neural-codec figures stay the 200-image proof above, which this run did not reproduce at
  scale. The raw per-image verdicts back every figure.

## Honest limits

The invisible keyed marks do not survive controllable regeneration from clean noise; the
opt-in visible seal (`--level maximum`) is built for exactly that case and survives it at a
measured cost in visibility, as the validation above records and `backfire/LIMITS.md`
documents. A center-crop past the mark's support is not read by the raw Backfire band alone.
Video writing is available only through the keyed Backfire path; the free product reads video
marks but does not embed them.
