# v1.4.0: feature-complete declaration

**Backfire image validation at scale, plus documentation consolidation.**

This is a validation-and-documentation release. No product behavior, wire format, or
public API changes: the provcheck verifier and creator kit stay Apache-2.0 and unchanged
in their guarantees, and Backfire and provcheck-mellin stay separate, opt-in, BUSL-1.1
tools that are never bundled into the shipped binary.

## Headline: the Backfire image proof extends to 200 photos

The v1.3.0 Backfire validation ran over a twenty-image set. v1.4.0 extends the same
battery to a 200-image COCO corpus, every read taken through the shipped reader at its
default threshold:

- Diffusion regeneration attack: **99.5%** (matched model and held-out).
- Neural-codec (VAE) re-encode: **94 to 97.5%** across four compression models the mark
  was never tuned against; an iterated double-pass, the hardest case in the set, holds
  **86.5%**, disclosed rather than dropped.
- Everyday handling: JPEG **99.0%** (q90) and **98.5%** (q50), half-size resize **98.0%**, and blur **97.0%**.
- **Zero false positives** over the 200 marked images and over a separate 1,000 unmarked
  images.

The chart, the raw per-image verdicts, and a plotter that recomputes the aggregate from
them ship in `backfire/repro/`, so the number is auditable rather than a transcribed
summary. The honest limit is unchanged: Backfire does not survive controllable
regeneration from clean noise.

## Documentation consolidation

The audio watermark survival-range and multi-family embed docs are trimmed to
shipped-only reality, the Backfire `LIMITS.md` is tightened, and the README's Backfire
numbers, changelog, and Status are brought into agreement at the 200-image figures.

## Repository housekeeping

Remaining internal working material is cleaned from the published tree, the public-content
guard is hardened against line-ending mismatches, and the release workflow is scoped so it
runs only on the development repository.

## Release readiness

The validation numbers above are product-true at the shipped reader threshold and their
data ships alongside the claim; the workspace builds and the test suite passes. This file
is the operator's affirmative declaration that the codebase is feature-complete for the
v1.4.0 line; the iterations that constitute its scope are the commits from v1.3.0 to this
tag.
