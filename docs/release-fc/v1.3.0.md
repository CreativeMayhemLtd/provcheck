# v1.3.0: feature-complete declaration

**Backfire 3.0: a keyed image mark that survives learned removal.**

This release advances Backfire, the experimental keyed image watermark introduced in
v1.2.0, from surviving a single diffusion purifier to surviving two AI regeneration
attacks at once. The provcheck verifier core stays Apache-2.0 and unchanged in its
guarantees; Backfire remains a separate, opt-in, BUSL-1.1 tool that is never bundled
into the shipped binary.

## Headline: Backfire 3.0

Earlier Backfire optimized the mark to be a fixed point of one diffusion purifier. 3.0
makes the mark a joint fixed point of two removers at once: a diffusion purifier and a
learned neural codec (a compressai variational autoencoder). Both attacks leave the keyed
identifier readable instead of erasing it.

- **Validated against the published removal tool, not a toy.** The claim behind
  "invisible watermarks are provably removable" is Zhao et al. (arXiv:2306.01953), whose
  `WatermarkAttacker` couples a Stable Diffusion regeneration attack with a
  compressai VAE attack. Across a twenty-image COCO set the keyed serial survives the
  diffusion regeneration attack 20 of 20 (including on a purifier it was never tuned
  against), survives the neural-codec (VAE) attack 19 to 20 of 20, and survives benign
  JPEG and resize 20 of 20, every read taken through the tool's own reader at its shipped
  threshold. Zero false positives over 1,000 unmarked images.
- **Honest by construction.** `backfire/LIMITS.md` discloses the wall: Backfire does not
  survive controllable regeneration from clean noise (CtrlRegen) at real attack strength.
  We beat the published tool, we do not claim to beat every possible attack.
- **Imperceptibility is 30 dB PSNR**, the operating point at which the survival numbers
  above hold at the shipped reader threshold.
- **On by default.** The neural-codec objective ships enabled (`--codec-eot`,
  `--codec-iter`); `compressai` joins the embed dependencies and the setup scripts.

## Unchanged surfaces

The `read` path and its golden vectors are unchanged from v1.2.0; the thirteen Backfire
unit tests pass. `provcheck --backfire-read`, the desktop Backfire tab, `provcheck-mellin`,
and the Keyed marks tab are unchanged. Licensing is unchanged: Backfire and provcheck-mellin
stay BUSL-1.1 (free for non-commercial use) or commercial, each version converting to
AGPL-3.0-or-later four years after its first public distribution.

## Repository housekeeping

Internal-only operations tooling and notes are removed from the public tree, and residual
internal naming and private filesystem paths are scrubbed from source comments, docs, and
the changelog. No product behavior changes.

## Release readiness

Backfire 3.0 is validated at the numbers above, product-true at the shipped reader
threshold, and its tests pass. This file is the operator's affirmative declaration that
the codebase is feature-complete for the v1.3.0 line; the iterations that constitute its
scope are the commits from v1.2.0 to this tag.
